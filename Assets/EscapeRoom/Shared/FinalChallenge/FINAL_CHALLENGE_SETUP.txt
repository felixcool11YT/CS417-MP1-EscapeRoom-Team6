Departure station

Scene: Assets/EscapeRoom/Scenes/MP1B_EscapeRoom.unity
Hierarchy: MP1C_DepartureStation

Sequence
Three room rewards open the inner blast door into the airlock. The timer continues.
The station is along the east wall before the stairs, facing the clear center aisle.
PACK: Put the EXIT first-aid kit and radio into their matching box slots.
PURGE: Select particulate, chemical, then radiation. Wrong input resets progress.
POWER: Set FILTERS ON, HATCH ON, AUX OFF, then press TEST to release the exterior hatch.
Climb the stairs, push the released hatch open, then cross the outside trigger to win.
The hatch and ending both reject attempts to bypass departure preparation.
Completed packing secures both supplies in the box. Restart resets the sequence.

Components
DepartureStationController.cs and DepartureStationController.Setup.cs form one
partial class. The first file declares the stages and filter sequence; the
second handles socket events, control gating, feedback and the door handoff.
BreakerSwitch and XRHoverHighlight are reused from the generator room.

01_PackDepartureKit contains the supplies, supply sockets and PackingStatus.
02_PurgeExitAir contains the controls, StationStatus text and FilterProgressLamps.
03_PowerOuterDoor contains the three BreakerSwitch components and TestButton.
04_R_Instructions opens 07_NoteReadingPanel through a serialized UnityEvent.
FinalDoorSystem/05_AuthorizationSign remains above the original three authorization sockets.
06_StationFeedback holds AcceptedAudio, RejectedAudio and CompletedAudio.
00_StationFrame/StationStatus displays the next action.
Each stage has a StatusLamp with neutral, ready and complete materials.

Supply interaction layers
ExitFirstAid and ExitRadio keep the departure supplies separate from the room
supplies and from each other. Mounted filter cartridges are secured behind guard bars. Use the labelled buttons below.

Physics
Dynamic scene masses range from 0.1 to 1.5 kg (15:1). The spawned keycard is 0.2 kg.

Testing and building
Assets/Editor contains the integration check and Quest build entry points.
MP1cBuildTools.WireAndTest checks the final sequence before building Android.
MP1cBuildTools.BuildQuest builds the already-wired scenes.
Output: ../the_room_info/MP1c_Quest_Test
Android build copies exclude the desktop XR simulator.
Headset controls, reach, readability, audio and full-room completion require
physical-device testing before submission.
