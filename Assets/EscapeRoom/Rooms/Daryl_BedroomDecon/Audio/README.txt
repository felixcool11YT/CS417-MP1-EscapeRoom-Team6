Audio for the bedroom/decon room.

AMB_LockdownAlarmLoop.ogg (19s loop)
  Soft two-tone lockdown alarm. Play this while the alarm is still active.
  Kept mellow on purpose so it can loop without getting annoying.

AMB_EerieDroneLoop.ogg (56s loop)
  Dark ambient drone for after the player silences the alarm.

Both files loop seamlessly with no click at the loop point.

License: generated from scratch for this project. No attribution needed,
no royalties, use freely.

Unity setup: add an AudioSource, assign the clip, turn on Loop and
Play On Awake. Alarm is mixed at -9 dBFS, drone at -6 dBFS; adjust
Volume on the AudioSource to taste.
